# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Argue about ideas, not people.

## Our standards

**Expected:**

- Being welcoming to people at every level of experience
- Giving feedback on the substance of a contribution, not the person making it
- Accepting correction gracefully — including maintainers
- Assuming a question is asked in good faith
- Disclosing commercial interests when they are relevant

**Not acceptable:**

- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language of any kind
- Gatekeeping — "if you have to ask, you're not ready", "just read the source", and their many variants
- Publishing others' private information
- Deliberately posting misleading security advice
- Using this repository to advertise, recruit, or drive traffic without disclosure

## Why gatekeeping is called out explicitly

Security has an unusually high tolerance for people being condescending to beginners, and it drives away exactly the people the field needs. This repository exists for people who are learning. A question that seems obvious to you is not obvious to someone three months in.

If you cannot answer a question kindly, do not answer it.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere someone is representing this project.

## Enforcement

Report problems by opening a confidential issue or contacting the maintainer via their GitHub profile. Reports are handled discreetly.

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions that violate this code, and may ban repeat or serious offenders.
